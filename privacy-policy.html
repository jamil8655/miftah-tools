Miftah Tools — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

Miftah Tools ("Miftah Tools", "we", "us", or "our") is an all-in-one digital tools application designed to help users work with documents, files, images, PDFs, and other digital content.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed, collected, processed, or shared when you use Miftah Tools.

1. Information We Collect

Miftah Tools is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Files and Documents

When you select a file, document, image, PDF, or other content for processing, the application may access that content only as necessary to provide the requested feature. Files selected by you are used to perform the requested operation, such as conversion, compression, editing, extraction, or other processing. Miftah Tools does not claim ownership of your files or their contents.

Information You Provide

If a feature requires an account or other information, we may process information that you voluntarily provide, such as an email address or account identifier. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is required for a specific feature and clearly disclosed to you.

2. How We Use Information

  • Providing requested tools and features
  • Processing selected files
  • Converting or modifying files
  • Saving or exporting user-requested results
  • Maintaining application functionality
  • Improving reliability and security
  • Providing advertising where applicable
  • Responding to support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

3. File Processing

Depending on the specific tool and implementation, processing may occur locally on your device or through a required online service. When a feature requires an external service to process content, relevant data may be transmitted to that service only to perform the requested operation. Users should avoid submitting highly sensitive or confidential information to any online processing feature unless they are comfortable with the processing required by that feature.

4. Advertising

Miftah Tools may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. Advertising providers may collect or process certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from network information, and information related to advertising interactions, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable settings, consent requirements, and regional regulations.

5. Device Permissions

Miftah Tools requests only permissions necessary for its functionality. These may include Internet access, network-state information, file/document selection, and storage compatibility on supported older Android versions where required for saving or handling files.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be shared with third-party service providers only when necessary to provide a requested service, operate the application, serve advertisements, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Miftah Tools. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Files processed locally on your device remain subject to your device's storage and file-management controls. If a feature processes information through an online service, retention may depend on the technical requirements of that service. Where applicable, users may request deletion of personal information associated with an account by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

Miftah Tools is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. We do not knowingly sell personal information belonging to children.
