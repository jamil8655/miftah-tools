All 103 MiftahTools PDF tools in one place: merge, split, compress, convert, edit, sign and protect PDF files. Everything runs in your browser, so your documents never leave your device.

What are PDF tools?

PDF tools are online utilities for working with PDF documents: combining files, splitting them apart, reducing their size, converting them to and from formats such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint and JPG, and editing their pages. This page brings together all 103 PDF tools on MiftahTools in one directory, grouped by task, so you can go straight to the exact tool you need. Every tool is free, works in your browser and requires no signup.

How the PDF tools work

Using any tool follows the same three steps. First, open the tool and choose the file from your device. The file is read locally by your browser, not sent to a server. Second, adjust the options the tool offers, such as page order, output format or compression level, and run it. Third, download the finished file. Because the processing happens on your own device with in-browser technology, there is no upload queue and no copy of your document stored elsewhere.

Why browser-based PDF tools are more private

Most online PDF services work by uploading your document to their servers, where it may be stored, at least temporarily, before you download the result. Contracts, identity documents, certificates and financial papers are exactly the files people hesitate to upload. MiftahTools takes the opposite approach: the tools are built to run on the device in front of you, so the document never travels across the internet. You get the convenience of an online tool with the privacy of offline software.

Frequently asked questions

Are these PDF tools really free?

Yes. Every PDF tool on this page is free to use, and none of them requires an account or signup. Open the tool you need, process your file and download the result.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. MiftahTools PDF tools run entirely in your web browser, so your files are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers. That makes them suitable for personal and confidential documents.

What can I do with these PDF tools?

You can merge, split, organise and compress PDFs, convert files to and from PDF (including Word, Excel, PowerPoint and image formats), edit and annotate pages, sign documents, protect files with a password and run OCR on scanned documents. The full list is grouped in the sections above.

Do I need to install any software?

No installation is needed. The tools work in any modern web browser, so you can use them on a computer, tablet or phone without downloading an app or plugin.

Can I compress a PDF for an online application form?

Yes. The Compress and Optimise section includes several compression tools that reduce PDF file size. This is commonly used when a form, portal or email service asks for a smaller document.

How is MiftahTools different from other PDF websites?

Two things stand out: your files never leave your device, because all processing happens in your browser, and the PDF tools are part of a wider library of 222 free tools, so the same site also covers images, text, converters and developer utilities with no signup.
