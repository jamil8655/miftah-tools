Free Image Tools Online

All 32 MiftahTools image tools in one place: compress, convert, resize, crop and edit images. Everything runs in your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

What are image tools?

Image tools are online utilities for changing pictures and graphics: making files smaller, converting them to a different format, resizing or cropping them, and improving how they look. This page collects all 32 MiftahTools image tools in one directory, grouped by task. Every tool is free, runs in your browser and needs no signup, which makes them handy for quick jobs that do not justify opening a full photo editor.

How the image tools work

Each tool follows the same simple flow. Open the tool and pick the image from your device; the image is loaded locally by your browser, not uploaded anywhere. Adjust the options, such as the output format, dimensions or compression level, and run the tool. Then download the finished image. Because the work happens on your own device, there is no waiting for uploads and no copy of your photo left on a server.

Why browser-based image tools are more private

Photos are personal: they can show people, places, documents and screens. Many online image sites process pictures by uploading them to a server first. MiftahTools instead processes images on the device in front of you, using in-browser technology, so your pictures stay with you. It is the convenience of an online tool with the privacy of an app that works fully offline-style on your own files.

Common image tasks

A frequent job is shrinking a photo so it fits the upload limit of an application form, job portal or messaging app; the compressors and the resizer handle that in seconds. iPhone owners convert HEIC photos to JPG so the pictures open everywhere. Website owners convert images to WebP and compress them so pages load faster. The passport photo maker helps prepare photos for applications, the background remover prepares product and profile pictures, and the OCR tools pull editable text out of photos of documents and notices.

Frequently asked questions

Are these image tools really free?

Yes. Every image tool on this page is free to use, with no account or signup required. Open a tool, process your image and download the result.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image tools run entirely in your web browser, so your photos and graphics are processed on your own device and never uploaded to our servers.

Which image formats do the tools support?

The tools cover the common web and photo formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, BMP, TIFF and SVG. You can convert between them, for example HEIC to JPG, PNG to WebP or JPG to PNG, and compress JPG, PNG and WebP files.

How can I make a photo small enough for an online form?

Use one of the compressors in the Compress Images section, or resize the image dimensions with the Image Resizer. Both approaches reduce file size, and because everything runs in your browser you can try again instantly until the file fits.

Do I need to install an app or photo editor?

No. The tools run in any modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone, so there is nothing to install and no plugin is required.

How is MiftahTools different from other image tool websites?

Your images never leave your device, because processing happens in your browser rather than on a server, and the image tools sit inside a library of 222 free tools covering PDFs, text, converters and developer utilities, all with no signup.
